✦ Our Story

A little kitchen,
a lot of love.

Nina started as a small dream — a home kitchen, a worn-out apron, and an obsession with getting the perfect croissant lamination. What began as weekend baking for friends and family grew into something far sweeter.

Every item that leaves our kitchen is made by hand, using locally-sourced ingredients and time-tested recipes passed down through generations — with a modern twist that keeps you coming back.
